Term	Meaning
Tenant	Customer/account that pays for the SaaS subscription
Business	A business owned/managed within the tenant
User	Person with an application login
Tenant User	User who has access to the tenant
Business User	User who has access to a specific business
Role	What the user is allowed to do


The application will enforce the rule
A user must be a member of a tenant before they can be assigned to one of that tenant's businesses.
We'll enforce that in the application/service layer, and we'll design our authorization layer around it.
